principal governing document of our group.

discipline, honesty, transparency, accountability, responsible
leadership, mutual respect and active participation, we commit
ourselves to protecting the interests of the group and every
member.
We therefore adopt this Constitution to establish clear
principles and procedures governing membership, contributions,

Together we plan.
Together we contribute.
Together we grow.
Together we build a legacy.
